Own a Slice of Premium Real Estate.

FractionHome allows you to buy fractional shares of a real-world property with BOT on BOT Chain. When the property owner deposits rent, every shareholder claims their pro-rata yield instantly on-chain.

🪙 Fractional Shares

Buy as few as one share at a fixed on-chain price. Transfer shares to any wallet, peer-to-peer.

💸 Passive Yield

Property managers deposit rent directly to the contract. Connect your wallet and withdraw your exact pro-rata share instantly.

🔗 Immutable Proof

Live on BOT Chain mainnet. Share balances, purchases and rent payouts are recorded on-chain and verifiable on BOTScan.
